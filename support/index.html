Support

EconoPulse is a macOS menu bar application for exploring global economic data from the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

Need help?

If you have a question, found a bug, or are experiencing a problem with EconoPulse, please contact us:

Email: econo.pulse.dev@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your macOS version
  • Your EconoPulse version
  • A short description of the problem
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if possible
  • Screenshots or other useful information

Where does EconoPulse get its data?

EconoPulse uses economic data provided by the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

Does EconoPulse require an account?

No. EconoPulse does not require you to create an account.

Can I use EconoPulse without Premium?

Yes. EconoPulse provides free functionality. Some features may be limited or available only through Premium.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open EconoPulse and use the Restore Purchases option in the Premium purchase interface. Purchases are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

Feature requests

We welcome suggestions about new indicators, chart features, sharing options, and other improvements.

Please send your suggestions to the support email above.

About EconoPulse

EconoPulse is an independent macOS application designed to make global economic data easier to explore and compare.

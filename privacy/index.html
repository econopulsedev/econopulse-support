Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

EconoPulse ("the App") is a macOS application developed by Vasili Arlou.

This Privacy Policy explains how EconoPulse handles information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

EconoPulse does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information directly from you.

The App does not operate its own user account system or maintain a personal user profile.

Economic Data

EconoPulse retrieves economic data from the International Monetary Fund (IMF) to provide economic indicators, country comparisons, charts, and other features.

Requests to external services may include technical information necessary for the network request, such as your IP address, which may be processed by the relevant service according to its own privacy policy.

EconoPulse does not control the privacy practices of external services.

Purchases

EconoPulse may offer Premium subscriptions and/or one-time purchases through the Apple App Store.

Payments and purchase processing are handled by Apple through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

EconoPulse does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Sharing and Export

EconoPulse allows you to export and share charts and animations.

When you choose to share content, the selected file is provided to the macOS sharing system and the service you select, such as AirDrop, Mail, or Messages.

Information shared through these services is handled according to the privacy practices of the selected service.

Local Data

EconoPulse may store application preferences and locally generated files on your Mac, such as selected countries, indicators, chart data, and temporary export files.

Temporary files created during export are intended for local use and may be removed after they are no longer needed.

Analytics and Advertising

EconoPulse does not use third-party advertising networks.

EconoPulse does not sell personal information.

Children's Privacy

EconoPulse is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

EconoPulse may communicate with third-party services required to provide its functionality, including the International Monetary Fund for economic data and Apple for App Store purchases and system services.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at the published Privacy Policy URL.
